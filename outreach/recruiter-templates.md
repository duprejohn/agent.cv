# Recruiter outreach templates
Drafts for the user to personalize and send. Claude fills the brackets from the profile; never send without approval.

## 1. LinkedIn connection note (300-character limit)
Hi [Name], I'm a [title] with [N] years in [field], looking at [target roles] ([remote/city]). I saw you recruit for
[practice area] and would love to be on your radar. Thanks!

## 2. Email to a specialist recruiter
Subject: [Target title] | [N] yrs [field] | [location/remote]

Hi [Name],
I'm a [title] who [one-line proof point with a number]. I'm looking for [target roles] in [industries], [remote/city],
targeting [pay range]. Resume attached. I'm working with a few agencies and applying directly, so please check with me
before submitting me anywhere. Happy to talk this week.

## 3. Contract / staffing agency
Same as 2, plus: available [hours/week], rate [range] (W-2 or 1099), start [date].

## 4. Keep-warm note (every 3–4 weeks)
Hi [Name], a quick update: [new skill, project or availability]. Still looking at [target roles]. Anything I can send
your way, like a referral? Thanks!

## 5. Reply when a recruiter pitches a role
Thanks, [Name]. Could you share the employer, pay range and work arrangement? If it fits, I'm happy to talk. Please
don't submit me until I've confirmed; I'll check it against roles I've already applied to.
