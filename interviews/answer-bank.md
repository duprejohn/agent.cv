# Answer bank: questions to prep for every interview
Every prep sheet includes tailored **Superpower** and **Growth edge** answers built from these.

## "What's your superpower?"
Name it plainly, prove it with one example, and say why it matters for this role. 45–60 seconds.

## "What's your growth edge?"
A real one, what you're doing about it, and evidence it's working. Never a disguised strength.

## "Tell me about yourself"
90 seconds: now → before → why this role.

## "Why are you looking?" / "Tell me about the gap"
One honest sentence, then what you did with the time, then why you're ready now.
